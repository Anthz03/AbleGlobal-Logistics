About Us

Comprehensive, innovative solutions for all your logistical needs.

ABLEGLOBAL Logistics is a fully-licensed international freight forwarding company and certified non-vessel operating common carrier that provides multi-modal transport services.

Established in 2009, we are licensed for air and ocean freight shipping, customs clearance and delivery, project cargo handling, buyer's consolidation, warehousing and all other logistics-related services. We handle shipments to any destination worldwide.
